How I Used AI

For this assignment, I used ChatGPT to:

- Brainstorm possible arguments before writing and find a direction connected to my own practice.
- Help organise the structure of the essay.
- Translate ideas and content that I had first developed in Chinese into English.

I also used Codex to format and proofread the Markdown document and shorten the essay to fit the word limit. Codex's built-in image generation tool produced UI concept mockups for CrowdEscape, but I decided not to include images in the final essay.

## An AI Output I Kept

I kept this sentence:

> Programming shows me what I have not decided yet.

It captures what I was thinking about in the CrowdEscape project: programming helps me notice that some design decisions that seem clear have not actually been defined. In particular, choosing a label such as “Less Crowded” does not yet explain how the system should choose a route.

## An AI Output I Rejected

I did not use this sentence:

> Programming helps designers understand the digital medium and translate vague intentions into computational systems.

The wording felt too abstract and did not sound like me. It describes a general benefit of programming, but it does not clearly express what I discovered through CrowdEscape. I chose simpler, more direct language that connected to my own design decisions.
